# Memoria TFG (Thesis Memory)

This repository serves as the official academic repository hosting the final thesis memory (Treball de Fi de Grau), structured reports, digital portfolios, and extensive developmental documentation for the botanical systems developed for the UAB Faculty of Biosciences.

## Overview

The thesis covers the comprehensive design, development, field validation, and usability analysis of the entire UAB botanical cataloging suite. It documents the core objectives, engineering methodologies, challenges faced, and the academic findings.

## Key Sections Documented

- **Requirements Engineering** — Extensive field interviews and process analyses with the UAB biology faculty to define standard data structures and observation requirements.
- **System Architecture** — Diagrams and reasoning behind the WordPress, mobile app, and client-side database schemas.
- **Field Study Analysis** — Quantitative and qualitative findings on how students interacted with the mapping and gallery tools during practical university lessons.
- **Usability Testing** — Extensive assessment of the user interfaces based on the SUS (System Usability Scale) framework.

## Document Index

1.  **Project Abstract** — Summary of academic aims and final software deliverables.
2.  **Introduction & Context** — Theoretical background in local citizen science and digital cataloging.
3.  **Materials and Methods** — Software engineering steps, technical choices, and agile iterations.
4.  **Results** — Launch and field trial data of the WordPress plugin and gallery tools.
5.  **Conclusions** — Academic achievements and future research recommendations.

> [!NOTE]
> All technical terms, system layouts, and field study logs are compiled in highly-readable Markdown formats to encourage open-access distribution of these academic results.
